MADE FOR YOUR GAME

Fits your game,
and the way you test.

TCG-T is not tied to one specific card game.
Set up the zones and card information, then move cards by your own rules.

01 / COLLECTION

Keep your cards and decks at hand.

Register from photos, files or a card ZIP. Organize names and attributes, then build decks.

02 / PLAY

Move cards and get a feel for it.

Draw, place, rotate. Track life, pause and resume Solo Play, replay past games and try a different line from any move, and play online with someone far away.

03 / CUSTOMIZE

Your board, your moves.

Configure zones, attributes and deck structure. Save the steps you repeat as quick actions.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start

Players decide card effects and who wins; the app does not judge rules. Card images and decks are stored on your device. Image autofill and creating an online table use points, which you can also earn by watching a video ad.

The app is available in English, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French and Portuguese.
