Pricing, points and Premium

What you can do for free, what points are for, Premium benefits, and restoring or cancelling purchases.

Updated: October 13, 2026 · Check the latest terms on the in-app purchase and action screens.
On this page
1. What is free and what uses points2. Check, buy and earn points3. Premium benefits and monthly price4. Auto-renewal and cancelling5. Restoring purchases and changing phones6. If a purchase or points do not show up← Back to the guide
Purchases are made in the app. Check prices, the products on sale and the points needed on the app screen right before buying or using them.

1. What is free and what uses points

What you want to doHow it works
Set up games, register images and ZIPs, build decks, play soloThe core features are free. You can enter card information by hand.
Draw odds and combo oddsThe basic calculators are free.
Autofill card names and attributes from images with AI1 point per card. No points are used if reading fails.
Create an online tableThe creator uses points. Check the points shown before creating. Joining is free.
Use sleeves and playmatsAvailable by exchanging points per target, or while Premium is active.

Creating a game setup by talking with an outside AI is separate from the in-app AI autofill that reads card images. Using an outside AI is subject to that service's own pricing and terms.

2. Check, buy and earn points

  1. Open "Balance" in the "Points" section of Settings.
  2. Check your current balance, and what was granted or used in "History".
  3. To buy, sign in, check the packs and store prices shown, and purchase.
How to get pointsDetails
Buy pointsPacks of 60, 200, 450 and 1,000 points. Check the products and prices on the purchase screen. Purchased points do not expire.
Video ads1 point for watching to the end. The daily limit and remaining count are shown on screen. Sometimes no ad is available.
Premium benefit150 points are granted each monthly purchase or renewal cycle.

Point packs are one-time purchases. Nothing is bought automatically when your balance runs low. 30-day decoration exchanges also work by extending when you want to keep going.

Sign in before buying. Your point balance is tied to your app account. Premium's monthly points go to the same account.

3. Premium benefits and monthly price

Premium is a monthly auto-renewing plan. You can check the current monthly price and terms under "Premium" in Settings.

  • No ads
  • Sleeves and playmats while active
  • 150 points every month
  • Keep up to 100 game histories (free version: latest 3)
AI autofill still costs 1 point per card with Premium, and creating an online table also uses points separately. Premium is not an unlimited AI or table plan.

You can choose to unlock decorations individually with points, or use them while Premium is active. For the differences in targets and periods, see the decorations guide.

Check the price the app loads from the store. If no price appears, check your connection and whether purchases are available in your environment.

4. Auto-renewal and cancelling

Premium renews automatically until you cancel. To stop renewal, use the subscription management of the store you bought from, and check the end date.

Store you bought fromHow to cancel
App StoreOn iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions and choose the subscription. Apple's official steps
Google PlayIn Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions and choose the subscription. Google's official steps

Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. Check the renewal date and active period in the store's management screen.

When Premium ends, decorations used through it end too. If a target still has valid point-exchange rights, you can use it within those.

5. Restoring purchases and changing phones

What you want backWhat to check or do
Premium statusMake sure you use the same store account as when you bought, then go to Settings → "Premium" → "Restore purchases".
Point balanceSign in to the same app account as when you bought, and refresh the points screen. "Restore purchases" does not re-grant points.
Cards, decks and decoration imagesThese are stored on your device. Signing in or restoring Premium does not move images or decks to another device.

Before changing phones or reinstalling, save the data you need elsewhere, for example by exporting a card ZIP. A card ZIP does not include everything, such as finished decks, boards and decoration images. See also what a card ZIP covers.

This restore does not guarantee moving Premium to a different store from the one you bought from, such as between iOS and Android. If you are unsure of your purchase status, contact support.

6. If a purchase or points do not show up

  1. Check the store account you bought with and the account you are signed in to in the app.
  2. For points, wait a moment, pull down on the points screen to refresh, and check your balance and history.
  3. For Premium, check the purchase status in the store, then try "Restore purchases".
  4. If that does not solve it, contact support with what you bought, when, your device and OS, the app version, and any message on screen.

While waiting for a purchase to show up, check your purchase history before buying the same product again. Do not send passwords or card numbers.
