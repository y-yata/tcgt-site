DECORATIONS

Make it look like your game, too.

Turn your favorite images into sleeves and playmats. How to set them up, how to unlock them, and what they apply to.

The screens show decoration examples from the demo. Decorations use images you provide yourself. Apply the steps to your own decks and board designs.

1. Dress up with sleeves and playmats

Put your favorite art on the back of your deck, or spread your game's world across the board. Use your own images to give your usual Solo Play the look you like.

Tap an image to enlarge it. The screens are demos for explanation.

What you can changeWhat you can set
SleeveThe back of the cards. Set an image for the whole deck or per pile.
PlaymatThe board background. Set images and opacity for your side and the opponent's side.

This is not a feature for buying images; it lets you use your own images as decorations. You do not need the demo images shown here.

2. How to unlock and what is covered

Decorations are available by exchanging points, or while Premium is active. Before exchanging, check the target deck or board design and the points shown on screen.

How to unlockPeriod and scope
30-day point exchangeUse decorations on the chosen target for 30 days. Extending before it expires adds to the time remaining.
Permanent point exchangeUse decorations on the chosen target with no time limit.
PremiumWhile active, use sleeves on every deck and playmats on every board design.
Point exchanges are per target. Sleeves are unlocked per deck, and playmats per board design. One exchange does not unlock every deck and board.

You do not need to exchange again to swap images on the same target. Using decorations through Premium is separate from a "Permanent" point exchange. After Premium ends, you can use decorations according to any point-exchange rights left on each target.

Prices and terms for points and Premium are shown on the in-app purchase screens. Check the final price, points needed and period before buying or exchanging.

3. Set a sleeve

  1. From the deck list, open the deck's "Details" and choose "Sleeve".
  2. Check whether it is unlocked. If not, check the target and period and exchange points, or check your Premium status.
  3. Choose "Whole deck" or a specific pile to apply it to.
  4. Pick an image from your photo library with "Choose image", and set the crop.
  5. Start a Solo Play with the deck and check how face-down cards look.

Cards in piles without their own image use the whole-deck sleeve. If the whole deck has none either, the default back is used.

To use an image from the cloud, save it to your device's photo library first. Picking an image for decorations uses a different entry point from the "File" import when registering cards.

4. Set a playmat

  1. Open the game's edit screen and choose "Playmat" on the board design you want. Save a new board first.
  2. Check the target board design and whether it is unlocked.
  3. Choose your side or the opponent's side, and set an image from your photo library with "Choose image".
  4. Adjust the opacity and "Fit" while watching the preview.
  5. Pick that board design in a deck, start a Solo Play, and check that cards and numbers are easy to read.

If you do not set an image for the opponent's side, your side's image is used. Fit and position are shared by both sides.

SettingWhen to use it
OpacityLower it when the background makes text or cards hard to read. Adjustable for each side.
Fill the tableFills the table with the image. Parts that overflow are cut off.
Keep ratioFits the whole image. There may be empty margins.
PositionWith "Keep ratio", choose top, center or bottom.

To line up card zones with frames printed in the image, choose "Keep ratio" and set the zones' reference to "Playmat" in the board design. Zones you want outside the image, such as the hand, can stay on "Table". See the advanced settings guide for details.

5. Change, remove or extend

Swap the image with "Change image", or remove it with "Remove". Removing a per-pile sleeve falls back to the whole-deck setting, and removing the opponent's playmat uses your side's image.

The images you set are kept even after the period ends. Once decorations are available again, your saved settings are used. To extend, check the target, points needed and the expiry date.

Removing an image and deleting a deck or board design are different actions. If you delete a target you exchanged points for, the unlock cannot be moved to another target and the points are not refunded. If you only want to change the look, swap or remove the image.

6. Choose colors for the whole app

Change the color scheme of the whole app with "Color theme" in Settings. This is separate from point exchanges for sleeves and playmats. Try picking a theme that keeps text and buttons easy to read against your backgrounds.

The check mark in the theme list shows the theme you are using.

If a setting does not apply

ProblemWhat to check
You cannot pick an imageWhether the target is saved, whether decorations are unlocked, and photo access permission.
A different sleeve appearsA per-pile setting takes priority over the whole-deck setting. Check what it is applied to.
A different background appearsWhether the board design the deck uses is the same one you decorated.
The edges of the image are cut offSwitch from "Fill the table" to "Keep ratio".
Frames and cards do not line upWhether the zones' reference in the board design is "Table" or "Playmat".
