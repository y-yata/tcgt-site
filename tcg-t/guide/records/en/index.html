BASICS

Keep your matches, and look back.

Log matches played outside the app, such as shop events, together with your solo play records, and check win rates, matchups and how far you can go in an event.

Updated: November 19, 2026 · For iOS and Android 1.0.7
What you see may differ depending on your device, OS and game setup.

Records, the dashboard, the win/loss matrix, the event simulator and JSON export are all free. Records stay on your device and are not sent to a server. You can also tap "?" at the top right of each screen for help.

1. Open match records

  1. Tap "Match records" on Home.
  2. Use the switch at the top to choose All, Solo play or Outside the app. Solo play is what you played on the board; Outside the app is shop events or matches with friends.
  3. Use "Game" to choose which game's records to see. It starts with the game selected on Home.

Records and deck tags are kept per game. Deleting a game also deletes its records and deck tags (the confirmation shows how many).

Your latest solo game appears as "Most recent game · kept automatically" on Home, where you can continue it or save it (solo play records).

2. Log a match played outside the app

Tap "+ Log a match" and enter one game at a time.

ItemWhat to enter
Your deckPick a saved deck, or just a deck tag. For a deck you have not saved, you can create a new deck tag on the spot
Opponent's deckPick or create a deck tag. Choose "Unknown" if you do not know
Result (required)Win, loss or draw
First or secondGoing first, going second, or not sure
EventEvent name (names you used before are suggested), date and round. Rank and number of players are entered once per event, and can be added later
Main reason for a lossOnly for losses, optional: bad draw, matchup, my misplay, opponent ran hot, or time ran out
OtherTwo free tags and a note. There is no field for the opponent's name or other personal details

After saving, "Log round N of (event)" appears and carries over the event name, date and your deck. For best-of-three, enter two or three games under the same round.

Matches played outside the app have no limit. This is separate from the number of saved solo play records (10 on free, 100 with Premium).

3. Browse and edit the list

Switch the list between "By match" and "By event".

  • By match: one game per row, with the opponent's deck art, both decks, date, first or second, event and round, and the result.
  • By event: one event per row, with the number of games, rank and players, and the record. Tap it to open the rounds, then use "+ Record round N" at the bottom to add the next round.

Narrow the list by period, event name and deck tags. The same filters also apply to the dashboard, the win/loss matrix and the event simulator. Tap a match played outside the app to edit or delete it.

4. Tidy up deck tags

"Deck tags ›" in the list shows all deck tags for the game.

  • Art: if a deck is linked, its main image is used. You can also pick an image from your saved cards or your photos.
  • Rename and merge: merging different spellings into one also updates the records.
  • Delete unused tags: switch to "Unused" to see tags used by no record and no deck. Delete them one by one or all at once (this cannot be undone).

5. Check win rates on the dashboard

Shows your overall, going-first and going-second win rates with win/loss counts, how often you met each opponent deck tag and your win rate against it, and a breakdown of the main reasons for losses.

  • By default it counts only matches outside the app. Solo play is mostly practice, so it is kept apart from real results. You can switch to solo play or all.
  • Narrow your deck tag to one to see that deck's results.
  • Win rate is wins ÷ (wins + losses). Draws are counted but not included in the rate. Pairings with fewer than 5 games are shown faded as "few".

6. See matchups in the win/loss matrix

A table of each row deck's win rate against each column deck.

  • It does not matter which side you played. Each game is counted from both sides, so A vs B and B vs A add up to 100%.
  • Choose which deck tags appear as rows and as columns. Switch the view between overall (first/second), going first and going second.
  • Cells above 50% get ▲ and a color, cells below 50% get ▼. Cells with fewer than 5 games get no color or mark. Tap a cell to list those games below.
  • "Image" at the top right turns the table, with the current filters, into one image you can save or share. Deck tag names and art appear as they are, so check before sharing.

7. Try an event in the simulator

Runs an event many times to see how far your deck is likely to go. Everything is calculated on your device.

  1. Pick your deck tag. Opponent shares and your going-first and going-second win rates against each opponent are filled in from your records. You can edit any number; edited values turn yellow.
  2. Pick a format: Swiss (no drop, drop after 2 losses, or drop after 3 losses) or elimination (single, double or triple).
  3. Set the number of players (4 to 10,000), rounds and runs (10, 100 or 1,000), then tap "Simulate".

In "Matchups between other players", tap a cell and edit it in the field below. Pairings your records cannot tell are shown as "—" and treated as even. Results show average wins, the chance to win or reach the top 8, the spread of wins and ranks, and one example run. Results are not saved.

8. Export as JSON

"JSON" in the list, the dashboard and the win/loss matrix exports the records that match your current filters. Copy them or share them as a .json file. You can also copy a ready-made request for AI; paste both into the AI you use to get tables or charts in any form you like. The app does not send anything to AI.

Deck tag art is not included in the export. Notes are included only if you choose to.
