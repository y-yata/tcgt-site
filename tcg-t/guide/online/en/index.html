BASICS

Far apart, at the same table.

From meeting at a table over a call to picking decks and playing.

What you see may differ depending on your device, OS and game setup.

The screens use demo cards. What you actually use are your own card images or a card ZIP you received. Substitute your own game name, card names and counts as you go. You do not need the demo data.

1. Get ready with your opponent

Set up a call or another way to talk with your opponent, and agree on the game you will play. There is no random matchmaking: you meet at the same table with a room code.

If you use your own deck, register it first. You can also borrow a deck your opponent lends, for that table only. Players confirm card effects and how the game proceeds between themselves.

3. Pick decks and get ready

  1. Choose your deck. The lending side can turn on "Lend your decks".
  2. To borrow, choose a deck your opponent is lending.
  3. You can join a table even if your opponent uses a different game. In that case, each of you plays on your own game's board with your own deck. Read the message if one appears.
  4. When both players tap "I'm ready", the match screen opens.
A borrowed deck is for that table only. It is not saved to your own card or deck lists.

Each player can use up to 3 decks at one table. To use more, create a new table.

When playing different games, there is only one shared zone in the middle for both players. If your shared zones are set up differently, cards in a zone only one side has are not visible to the other.

4. Check what each side sees

The same match seen by each player (screens shown in Japanese). Tap an image to enlarge it.

What is revealed, such as the hand, follows the board's settings. Check not only face up or face down but also the "What your opponent sees (online)" setting. For how to set it, see the advanced guide.

Move your own cards and check that they update on your opponent's side as you go. If the connection drops, check the connection status and the message on screen, and pause until you and your opponent are in sync.

Tap a card to choose rotate or flip. "View" does not appear for your opponent's hidden deck or hand. Move cards on the board by dragging.

In games that count life as a number, you can also change your opponent's life (for play styles where the attacking side applies damage). When you do, your opponent is notified on their screen. Your opponent's actions are also shown to you in speech bubbles.

5. End the match

Read the confirmation and tap "Leave this table" to finish. Tables are for two players, so once it closes your opponent cannot continue at that table either.

Card images uploaded to the server for the match are deleted when the table closes. A table left open closes 3 hours after it was created.

You cannot resume an online board from "Suspended games" in Solo Play.

To leave during a match, open the confirmation from end at the top of the board. The × inside the log or tools panel only closes that panel.
