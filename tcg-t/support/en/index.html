FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQ

Does the app come with cards or decks?

No. You register your own game and cards. The demo screens on this site are only there to explain how things work.

I imported a QR code, but there are no cards.

A QR code shares a game setup only. Register card images or a card ZIP separately, then build a deck.

Do I have to use AI to register cards?

No. You can enter everything by hand. Image autofill is optional and uses points.

If I sign in, do my cards and decks move to my other devices?

No. Cards and decks are stored on your device, and signing in does not sync them to the cloud. A card ZIP is not a backup of the whole app either.

Do I need two decks for Solo Play?

No. You can pick the same deck for both yourself and the opponent.

Can I save an online match and resume it later?

Online matches work differently from Solo Play: you cannot reopen an online board from "Suspended games". The flow of the match is kept in "Game history", so you can replay it afterwards.

Can I try a different line from the middle of a past game?

For Solo Play, yes. On the replay screen, tap "Continue from here" to start a new Solo Play from the board at that move. The original history stays unchanged. Online match histories can only be replayed. See the Solo Play guide for details.

For sleeves and playmats, see the decorations guide for how to set them up and what they apply to.
