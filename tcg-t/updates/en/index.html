UPDATES

Updates

Changes in upcoming and released versions, and confirmed issues.

Available now

1.0.7

Released on iOS and Android on November 20, 2026.

  • Added "Match records". Log matches played outside the app, such as shop events, on the same screen as your solo play records, and review them with the Dashboard (win rates, first or second, by opponent, main reasons for losses), the Win/loss matrix (a matchup table between deck tags that you can share as an image) and the Event simulator (Swiss and elimination). Records can also be exported as JSON. Tap "?" on each screen for help (match records guide).
  • Export and import a single deck as a ZIP together with its game setup, cards and board design.
  • Put your own photo behind QR share images, and added "Post to X" and "Send via LINE".
  • Added "Board", "Playmat" and "Quick actions" to each game in the game setup list. "Create quick actions with AI" moved to the quick actions screen.
  • Refreshed the look and added "Clear Contrast", a theme designed for color vision differences.
  • Continue or save your most recent solo game from Home.

1.0.6

Released on iOS and Android on November 16, 2026.

  • An envelope button at the top right of each tab’s main screen opens News. Read Gifts, Updates and Other announcements in a list and detail view. A distribution notice can open the coupon entry screen with its code filled in.
  • Video ad rewards changed: 3 points for the first ad of each day (enough for one online table), then 1 point for each of the second through fifth ads. Up to five ads per day; a new day starts at midnight Japan time (JST).
  • Aligned the position and size of the title row on the main screen of all five tabs.

1.0.4

Released on iOS and Android on November 13, 2026.

  • Added Coupons in Settings. Enter a free code distributed by us to receive coupons for using sleeves or playmats for 30 days, or creating one online table. Coupons are separate from points and expire six months after you receive them.

1.0.3

Released on iOS and Android on November 10, 2026.

  • "Suspended games" and "Game history" are now together in "Solo Play records". Choose whether to save when you exit; the latest game is kept automatically. Save up to 10 records for free or 100 with Premium. Find them by tags or results, continue, replay, or branch. You can also replay online match records.
  • Edit deck names and deck tags directly in the deck list and filter by tag. Active sleeves appear behind deck covers.
  • After creating an online table, use "Send an invite" to share the room code and store links.
  • Fixed a crash after editing and saving from deck details, a board shrinking when a game has shared zones, and a Solo Play record sometimes missing immediately after an app crash.

1.0.2

Released on iOS and Android on November 4, 2026.

  • When you close the first-run guide, a generic "Sample" game is now set up for you, so you can start by adding cards without creating a game first.
  • Fixed an issue where typing a zone name in Japanese in the board settings could commit text in the middle of conversion.

1.0.1 (first release)

Released on iOS and Android on October 31, 2026. Future changes and confirmed issues will be posted on this page.

Please send problem reports and questions to support.
